# Aozora Trading Co., Ltd. — Company Profile

## 1. About Us

Aozora Trading Co., Ltd. is a wholesale distributor of household goods, founded in 1998. Guided by our corporate motto, "Good things for everyday life, always close at hand," we supply daily-living merchandise to retailers, mass merchandisers, and mail-order businesses across Japan.

- Company name: Aozora Trading Co., Ltd.
- Founded: April 1998
- Head office: 1-2-3 Aozora, Kakuu-ku, Tokyo
- Branch offices: Osaka and Fukuoka (two locations)
- Employees: 142 (as of April 1, 2026)
- Annual revenue: 3.8 billion yen (fiscal year 2025)
- Business: Wholesale of household goods

## 2. Product Categories

Our merchandise is organized into four categories:

1. **Kitchenware** — Cooking utensils, storage containers, tableware, and kitchen consumables. This has been our core business since the company's founding and accounts for roughly forty percent of sales.
2. **Bath and Toiletries** — Bathing goods, washroom items, towels, and cleaning supplies. We also have an extensive record of deliveries to hotels and other lodging facilities.
3. **Storage and Organization** — Storage cases, hangers, shelving, and closet accessories. Our strength lies in proposal-based assortments that follow changes in living spaces.
4. **Seasonal Goods** — Cooling items for summer, warming goods for winter, and merchandise for year-end and New Year events. We support seasonal sales-floor planning from the earliest stages.

We source from approximately 320 suppliers in Japan and overseas, and handle only products that meet our quality inspection standards.

## 3. Locations

- **Head Office (Tokyo)**: 1-2-3 Aozora, Kakuu-ku, Tokyo. Responsible for corporate management, product planning, and sales in eastern Japan.
- **Osaka Branch**: Opened in 2005 as our sales base for western Japan, focusing on transactions with mass merchandisers in the Kansai region.
- **Fukuoka Branch**: Opened in 2012 as our sales base for the Kyushu region, deepening relationships with local retailers.

## 4. History

- 1998: Aozora Trading Co., Ltd. founded in Kakuu-ku, Tokyo; began wholesale of kitchenware
- 2003: Entered the bath and toiletries field
- 2005: Opened the Osaka branch
- 2010: Added storage and seasonal goods, establishing the current four-category structure
- 2012: Opened the Fukuoka branch
- 2020: Introduced the in-house inventory system "Aozora Inventory Ledger," digitizing stock management
- 2025: Reached annual revenue of 3.8 billion yen

## 5. Our Commitment

With 142 employees working across our head office in Tokyo and our branch offices in Osaka and Fukuoka, we aim to be a trusted partner for every customer. We will continue to refine our four product categories and our logistics quality so that good everyday goods reach store shelves reliably, season after season.
